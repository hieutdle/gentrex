Technical methods

Target regions are captured from the genomic DNA of the submitted sample with hybridization probes and then sequenced on a next-generation sequencing platform. Read depth is used to call deletions and duplications of exons. {{ hgvs_example or "" }}

Limitations

No test finds every variant, so a pathogenic variant in the genes analyzed is still possible after a negative result. The test may miss variants in regulatory regions, deep in introns or in regions of high homology.
