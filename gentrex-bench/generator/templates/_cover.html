About This Report

The report that follows begins with the patient and specimen details and the overall result. The results section lists each reportable variant with its classification. The interpretation explains what the result means for the patient and for relatives, and the recommendations suggest next steps.

Each variant is assigned to one of the five ACMG classes: pathogenic, likely pathogenic, uncertain significance, likely benign or benign. The report leaves out likely benign and benign variants.

Clinical decisions should not be based on a variant of uncertain significance. The laboratory reviews such variants as new evidence appears and issues an amended report when a classification changes.

The methods and limitations at the end of the report explain what the test can and cannot detect. A genetic cause of the patient's condition remains possible after a negative result.
