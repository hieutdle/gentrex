Methods and Limitations

Genomic DNA is hybridized to a whole-genome SNP microarray with copy number and SNP probes. Copy number is called from probe intensity and allele ratios, and positions are given on the GRCh38 reference genome. The array detects deletions and duplications of about 50 kb and larger, and regions of homozygosity of 5 Mb and larger. It does not detect balanced rearrangements, sequence variants or low-level mosaicism.
