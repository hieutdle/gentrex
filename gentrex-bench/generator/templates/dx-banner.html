Methods

  • Target regions are captured from the genomic DNA of the submitted sample with hybridization probes and then sequenced on a next-generation sequencing platform. Each target is sequenced to a depth of at least 50x unless the report states otherwise.
  • Reads are aligned to the GRCh38 reference sequence. Sequence changes are reported according to HGVS nomenclature. {{ hgvs_example or "" }}
  • To find deletions and duplications of exons, software developed by the laboratory estimates the copy number of each target by comparing its read depth in the patient's sample with the average depth of the other samples in the run.

Limitations

In validation studies, analytical sensitivity and specificity both exceeded 99% for single nucleotide variants, for insertions and deletions shorter than 15 bp, and for deletions and duplications of one exon or more. Variants in regions with poor coverage, in deep intronic sequence or in repetitive sequence may be missed by this assay.

Disclaimer

A DNA test alone cannot settle the diagnosis of the selected condition(s) in every individual. Errors are also possible, for example from a mix-up of samples or from a mistake in the interpretation of the data.
