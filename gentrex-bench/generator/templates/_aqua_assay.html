Methods: DNA is extracted from the submitted specimen with a commercial kit, and its concentration is measured. {% if kind == "exome" %}Coding exons and flanking intronic sequence of nearly all human genes are captured with a commercial exome kit and sequenced on a massively parallel sequencing platform to a mean depth of at least 100x. {% else %}The coding exons and at least 5 bases of flanking intronic sequence of each gene are enriched by hybridization capture and sequenced on a massively parallel sequencing platform. {% endif %}Reads are aligned to the GRCh38 reference genome, and variants are called and filtered with a validated bioinformatics pipeline. Large deletions and duplications are identified from read depth and verified with a second, independent method. Variants are classified with the ACMG guidelines. {{ hgvs_example or "" }}

Limitations: This test detects single nucleotide variants, small insertions and deletions, and deletions or duplications of one exon or more in the regions analyzed. It may not detect variants in regulatory regions, deep intronic variants, low-level mosaicism, repeat expansions, or structural rearrangements such as inversions. Interpretation relies on the clinical and family history provided and on the literature at the time of reporting. Classifications may change as new evidence becomes available.
